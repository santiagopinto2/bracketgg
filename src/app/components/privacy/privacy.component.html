BracketGG shows public tournament data from start.gg.

If you log in with start.gg

Logging in is optional. If you do:

  • The site asks start.gg for one permission, to see your start.gg identity (user.identity).
  • The site's server keeps your start.gg login so you stay logged in. Your browser gets a session cookie for that. There are no tracking or ad cookies.
  • The site uses your login only to load start.gg data for you.
  • Logging out deletes your login from the server.

Light or dark

If you switch the theme, your browser remembers the choice. If you're logged in, the server keeps it with your login too.

Caching

The server keeps public answers from start.gg for a few seconds to a few minutes, so it sends start.gg fewer requests.

What the site doesn't do

No analytics and no ads. Nothing is sold or shared.
